## The active source route and the earlier attribution correction

The pinned official [VICE3.10 source archive](../../build/research/quondam/hardware-source/vice-3.10.tar.gz) selects `c64/c64cpusc.c` and `c64/c64memsc.c` for `libc64sc`. The CPU wrapper includes `mainc64cpu.c`, which includes **6510dtvcore.c**. The generic **6510core.c** and `c64mem.c` are not the compiled x64sc route. The new [STORE source receipt](../../build/research/aloft-pixels-20261109/display-analysis/source-route-01/store-route-proof.json) and [exact line atlas](../../build/research/aloft-pixels-20261109/display-analysis/source-route-01/source-route-anchors.json) make that correction explicit; frozen earlier artifacts remain unchanged.

The actual SC core also tests execution breakpoints before flushing watchpoints. Thus the older observed deferred watch drains remain valid observations, and that ordering is independently confirmed in the active source. What changes is the source attribution for instruction-cycle placement: SC `SET_ABS` and `SET_ZERO` call STORE **before** their final `CLK_INC`. The generic core's `CLK_ADD`-before-STORE macro must not supply SC timing.

For the admitted direct stores, completion C therefore gives a conservative visibility interval **[C−1,C]**, with explicit absence of an interrupt, trap, RAM-writing DMA or clock-changing alarm diversion between the callback and the completion context. `CLK_INC` advances the clock and then calls `vicii_cycle`; the existing phi1 has already occurred before the callback, and the next phi1 follows the increment. The row-staging consumer can consequently distinguish the next absolute write at c55–56 from the preceding last glyph fetch at c54. The other three writes complete at c0/3/6, before the first glyph fetch at c15. These are source counter/phase bounds, not electrical bus timestamps.

SC INC/DEC retains operand-read, old-value dummy STORE, final STORE order, with the three callback counters C−3/C−2/C−1 under the same no-diversion condition. Its queued hooks may all display completion C. The active SC `$04` and `$0C` NOP routes also perform dummy target reads; the earlier frozen generator/math lanes had left their physical read behavior outside their model. This new source note supplies that narrower implementation fact without rewriting those proofs.

The [root replay report](../../build/research/aloft-pixels-20261109/display-analysis/source-route-root-replay-check.json) independently repeats all four source-route outputs, 57,860 bytes, exactly. The retained [current installation metadata](../../build/research/aloft-pixels-20261109/environment-analysis/current-install-01/observer-environment.json) supports the source selection: the Homebrew recipe pins this archive and declares no patch. It was collected after the capture; its current executable hash is not presented as a capture-time executable identity or a local rebuild proof.

## Glyph fetch to committed pixels

MOS8565 selects the PAL 63-cycle table with `color_latency=0`. The compiled draw-visible flag comes from phi2 FetchC, not the table's separate `Vis()` annotation. It is true at zero-based cycles14–53. `cycle_flags_pipe` delays that flag one cycle, aligning the graphics pipeline's input with the actual phi1 glyph fetches at c15–54.

For column0, c15 fetches the source-derived byte and places it in pipe0 after drawing. At c16 it moves to pipe1. At c17, XSCROLL0 loads pipe1 into the shift register at pixel0 and renders eight pixels. `draw_colors_8565` writes the preceding pixel-buffer group before latching this new rendered group; the column therefore enters `dbuf` at **c18**. The last column follows at c57. The line buffer resets at c1; c18 begins at dbuf offset136. The normal-viewport copy subtracts `17*8−32=104`, putting column0 at x32. Forty columns fill x32–351.

For cbuf8, the four MSB-first two-bit symbols select `[D021,D022,D023,cbuf&7]`; each selected color occupies two physical pixels. The color-bit buffer makes the fourth color zero. Normal text's hires path, bitmap/ECM modes, XSCROLL changes, sprite composition and transient palette writes are outside this specialized contract.

The CSEL1 left-border comparator clears main-border at c16. Its old border latch still covers that cycle's rendered group, then updates; c17 graphics pass. The right comparator sets main-border at c56; the old open latch passes column39 before updating, so its delayed output at c57 remains visible. The next output group starts the right border at x352. A preceding right edge closes the border latch, and the long invisible/left-border prefix removes old shift and pixel-pipe contents before any retained viewport pixel. The finite checks cover every input byte at every column and four displaced incoming-byte seeds; source overwrite/latch reasoning supplies the general independence argument rather than treating four seeds as exhaustive state enumeration.
